Accuracy & sensor limits

Phone-local research, not certified navigation. GNSS anchors position; fresh gravity-free acceleration and Earth-referenced orientation support short gaps. A regularized bias model learns from this session's GNSS velocity changes after 12 valid windows. No KITTI model runs here. If sensors or speed/heading are missing, it coasts briefly or holds. Phone handling and compass errors cause drift. Keep the phone stable and the page foreground. Don't use while driving. No route is saved; map tiles reveal the viewed area to OpenStreetMap.
