Accuracy & sensor limits

Phone-local research, not certified navigation. GNSS anchors position; fresh gravity-free acceleration and Earth-referenced orientation support short gaps. A regularized bias model learns from this session's GNSS velocity changes after 12 valid windows. No KITTI model runs here. Research estimates last at most 20 seconds; after 8 seconds they are marked low confidence. The 25-second test holds for its final 5 seconds. This is a duration limit, not validated accuracy. If sensors or speed/heading are missing, it coasts or holds. On Safari, hold near level briefly to anchor compass alignment; full rotation then compensates tilt for up to 60 seconds since the last valid anchor. This assumes a stable browser yaw frame and still needs physical validation. A causal 120ms filter suppresses some vibration; large shocks and fast rotation reject motion for 250ms. Sustained bumps, turns, sensor bias and phone handling can still cause large drift. These gates are not validated on Indian roads. The displayed GPS radius is phone-reported; the model radius is illustrative, not actual error. Keep the phone stable and the page foreground. Don't use while driving.
